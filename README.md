## ClickMeeting Reviews

Real talk: ClickMeeting scores well with users, but it's not perfect. Here's what actual customers say.

### What Users Love

**Easy to Use**: Multiple reviewers consistently mention the intuitive interface. One user on G2 noted, "The most important reason I'd recommend ClickMeeting, apart from the obvious benefits of platform reliability/stability, is the fact that these people care!" The platform doesn't require technical expertise to get started—just create an account and you're hosting webinars within minutes.

**Reliability**: Users hosting large events (1,000+ attendees) report stable connections with minimal technical issues. A Capterra reviewer shared, "I've been using the app since the beginning of the pandemic... I was able to organize events with over 4,000 people online live." The browser-based approach means fewer variables that can go wrong compared to downloadable software.

**Customer Support**: ClickMeeting's support team gets high marks. Users mention quick response times and genuinely helpful assistance. One reviewer stated their support team "goes far and beyond what was agreed upon and personally cares about your success." Support operates during business hours with multiple contact options: email, live chat, and phone.

**Automation Features**: The Automated plan's ability to run webinars on autopilot while you're doing something else hits the mark for busy professionals. Users appreciate setting up evergreen content that generates leads around the clock.

**Recording Quality**: Sessions record in high quality, and the platform makes it simple to share recordings with attendees or keep them in your content library.

**Breakout Rooms**: For training and interactive sessions, the breakout room feature lets you split up to 800 attendees into 20 separate rooms, each supporting up to 40 people with full audio and video.

### Common Complaints

**Occasional Lag**: Some users report minor connectivity issues or lag during peak usage, especially with larger audiences. This seems related to internet connection quality more than the platform itself, given ClickMeeting's browser-based nature.

**Limited Customization**: While you can add logos and colors, some users want deeper branding control over the interface. The customization options work fine for most purposes but aren't as extensive as some competitors.

**Learning Curve for Advanced Features**: Basic webinars are straightforward, but diving into automation workflows and advanced settings can be confusing initially. Some reviewers mention the platform becomes "progressively complex" with numerous options that aren't always clearly explained.

**Pricing for Small Users**: Monthly pricing without discounts can feel steep for solo entrepreneurs or very small teams who only occasionally host webinars. The annual discount helps, but requires committing to a year upfront.

**Integration Limitations**: While ClickMeeting integrates with major tools (Moodle, HubSpot, Slack, Google Analytics, GetResponse, Keap, Pipedrive, Zapier), some users wish for more native integrations with appointment booking systems and additional CRM platforms.
